Says that a claim that the score predicts benefit from anti-PD-1 would need a comparator arm not given anti-PD-1 (preferably randomized) and a treatment-by-score interaction, meaning the association between score and response must differ between the arms. Plain wording counts and the words "predictive" and "prognostic" are not required, but saying only that a comparison group is needed, without the requirement that the association differ between arms, does not pass.
