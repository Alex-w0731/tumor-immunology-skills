Says that an internal estimate from these 40 patients is valid only if both label-dependent steps, the differential-expression selection of the 12 genes and the Youden choice of the cut-off, are redone inside every training fold of a cross-validation, with performance measured on the held-out patients only. Recommending only an independent cohort, or cross-validating the already-selected 12-gene score, does not pass.
