# CD39 as a marker of tumour-reactive CD8+ T cells (lab wiki draft, written by a literature agent)

- CD103+CD39+ CD8+ tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes are enriched for tumour-reactive cells across
  six malignancies, and a higher frequency of these cells is associated with better overall
  survival in head and neck cancer [1].
- Many CD8+ TILs in human lung and colorectal cancers recognise epitopes unrelated to cancer, such
  as Epstein-Barr virus, cytomegalovirus or influenza epitopes, and these bystander cells lack
  CD39 [2].
- In tumour-induced regulatory T cells, FoxP3 acts with STAT3 as a co-transcription factor that
  sustains IL-10 expression [3].
- A pan-tumour T-cell-inflamed gene expression profile, defined in 220 patients with 9 cancers,
  predicts clinical benefit from pembrolizumab [4].
- Antibodies blocking CD39 have produced objective responses as monotherapy in phase 1 trials.
