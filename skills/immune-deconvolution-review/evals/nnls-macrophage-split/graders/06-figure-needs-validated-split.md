The answer advises against putting an M1/M2 or M2 group difference in the grant figure, and its reason is not only the three-versus-three sample size: it says the M1 versus M2 split itself must first be shown to work, for example on mixtures of known composition or by IHC or flow cytometry of macrophage subsets.
