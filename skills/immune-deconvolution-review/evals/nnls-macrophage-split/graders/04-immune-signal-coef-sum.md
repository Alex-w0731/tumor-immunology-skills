From a fit on linear-scale values (log2(TPM+1) converted with 2^x - 1), the reply states that the non-responders carry less total immune signal than the responders and quotes the sum of the raw coefficients for both groups: about 0.35-0.41 in N1-N3 against 0.56-0.64 in R1-R3 with the bundled script's default row scaling, or about 0.26-0.33 against 0.49-0.58 for an NNLS fit without row scaling. Fractions alone, which sum to 1 in every tumour, do not pass, and neither does a fit on the log values (coefficient sums near 0.04-0.07).
