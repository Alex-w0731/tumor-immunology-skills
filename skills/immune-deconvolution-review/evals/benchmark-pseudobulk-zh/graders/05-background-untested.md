The answer notes that the benchmark measures background (estimates where a cell type is absent) only for Tregs, because CD8 T cells, CD4 T cells, B cells and macrophages are present in every mixture (lowest true share about 0.04 to 0.05 of all cells, 0.08 to 0.13 of the five types), and it recommends adding mixtures that lack each of these cell types, or low-abundance spike-in series, to the redesigned benchmark. Recommending only held-out cells or a patient-level split does not pass.
