The reply draws a usage decision for B cells: their estimates can be used to compare samples but not reported as fractions (or only after recalibration), because on the common denominator they rank the mixtures almost perfectly (r about 0.997) yet overestimate the true share by about 0.10 (mean 0.30 against 0.20, slope about 1.38). Identifying the overestimate without saying how B-cell values may or may not be used does not pass.
