The answer explains that CIBERSORT relative-mode fractions are proportions of the immune cells represented in LM22 (they sum to one per sample), so a lower CD8 fraction in SPP1-high tumours does not show fewer CD8 T cells, and it proposes an absolute-scale estimate (CIBERSORT absolute mode, EPIC or quanTIseq) or an immune-content estimate for that claim.
