The reply states that with 5 versus 5 patients an exact two-sided rank-sum (Mann-Whitney) test cannot give p below 2/252, about 0.008, and that this is above the threshold a correction over 78 pairs sets for the smallest p-value (0.05/78, about 0.00064, for Bonferroni or the first BH step), so the full 78-pair screen can hardly yield significant pairs and a few pairs should be pre-specified.
