The Treg-CD8T p-value the reply gives for the legend is a permutation p stated with the number of permutations m and is not smaller than 1/(m + 1) one-sided or 2/(m + 1) two-sided (for example p = 0.001, or p <= 0.001, with 999 permutations). A p of zero, or a normal-approximation p converted from the z-score of about 5 (around 10^-6 to 10^-7), does not pass.
