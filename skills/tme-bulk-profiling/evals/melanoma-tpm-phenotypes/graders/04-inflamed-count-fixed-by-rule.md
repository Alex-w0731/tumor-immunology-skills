The reply states that the tmelens rule calls inflamed every tumour at or above the 2/3 quantile of the infiltration axis, so 4 of 12 inflamed is fixed by the threshold (any 12 tumours with distinct scores give 4) and must not be reported as the cohort's prevalence of inflamed tumours. Presenting an inflamed percentage with a confidence interval, or one obtained from a different cut-off, as the cohort's inflamed fraction does not pass.
