The written reply gives a spread for at least one depth-matched overlap value (for example the SD, or the 2.5 to 97.5 percentiles over repeats, about 28% to 62% for the tumour-side share with 50 to 100 repeats) and describes it as variation between subsamples, not as a confidence interval for the two repertoires. Fail if no spread is given, or if it is called a confidence interval.
