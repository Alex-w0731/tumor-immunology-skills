The response applies an explicit minimum number of cells per pseudobulk, states the threshold, names the samples that fall below it and gives the number of patients per subtype that remain; with a 10-cell threshold this means T3 (6 cells) and H2 (4 cells) drop out, leaving 3 TNBC and 3 HR+ patients.
