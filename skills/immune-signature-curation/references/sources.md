# Sources for immune-signature-curation

Every source key used in `SKILL.md` and the other files under `references/` resolves here; the
scripts cite the same keys in their docstrings. DOIs and PMIDs were checked against Crossref,
PubMed and Europe PMC in November 2026. The indented line under each entry says what this skill
takes from the source and where that was checked (abstract or full text). Software and database
entries were read or queried directly.

## Primary literature

- **[Abeysooriya2021]** Abeysooriya M, Soria M, Kasu MS, Ziemann M. Gene name errors: lessons not learned. PLoS Comput Biol. 2021;17(7):e1008984. doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008984. PMID: 34329294.
  Gene name errors in 30.9% (3,436/11,117) of PubMed Central articles (2014-2020) with supplementary Excel gene lists; conversions to dates, floating-point numbers and five-digit internal date serials (abstract). Excel and Google Sheets converted gene names in all three import modes tested (opening a text file, pasting, typing), LibreOffice and Gnumeric did not; formatting the destination cells as plain text before pasting or typing avoided it; DEC1 is among the names converted to dates; the screen recognised date formats such as 3/1/2016, Mar-3 and 3-Mar (full text).
- **[Ambroise2002]** Ambroise C, McLachlan GJ. Selection bias in gene extraction on the basis of microarray gene-expression data. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2002;99(10):6562-6566. doi:10.1073/pnas.102102699. PMID: 11983868.
  Error rates are biased when the genes are selected on samples later used to test the rule, or when cross-validation is not external to gene selection; correct by repeating selection inside each fold of 10-fold cross-validation, or with the .632+ bootstrap external to selection (abstract).
- **[Aran2015]** Aran D, Sirota M, Butte AJ. Systematic pan-cancer analysis of tumour purity. Nat Commun. 2015;6:8971. doi:10.1038/ncomms9971. PMID: 26634437.
  Tumour purity confounds correlation and clustering of tumours with transcriptomic data (abstract).
- **[Ayers2017]** Ayers M, Lunceford J, Nebozhyn M, et al. IFN-gamma-related mRNA profile predicts clinical response to PD-1 blockade. J Clin Invest. 2017;127(8):2930-2940. doi:10.1172/JCI91190. PMID: 28650338.
  The T cell-inflamed gene expression profile was developed into a clinical-grade assay (abstract); its score was computed as the weighted sum of the housekeeping-normalised values of the 18 genes (full text, PMC article page). Used here as the example of a published gene list whose validated form is a weighted assay, not a mean of the listed genes.
- **[Barbie2009]** Barbie DA, Tamayo P, Boehm JS, et al. Systematic RNA interference reveals that oncogenic KRAS-driven cancers require TBK1. Nature. 2009;462(7269):108-112. doi:10.1038/nature08460. PMID: 19847166.
  Origin of single-sample GSEA (ssGSEA), as attributed by [Hanzelmann2013]; the method is not described in the abstract.
- **[Bruford2020]** Bruford EA, Braschi B, Denny P, Jones TEM, Seal RL, Tweedie S. Guidelines for human gene nomenclature. Nat Genet. 2020;52(8):754-758. doi:10.1038/s41588-020-0669-3. PMID: 32747822.
  Symbols must only contain upper-case Latin letters and Arabic numerals (Table 1), and the 'orf' of C#orf# symbols is written in lower case; readthrough transcripts join parent symbols with a hyphen (INS-IGF2); the rules are guidelines, and numerous exceptional legacy symbols remain approved; symbols that affect data handling, including all that auto-converted to dates in Microsoft Excel, have been changed (SEPT1 is now SEPTIN1, MARCH1 is now MARCHF1) (full text, PMC author manuscript).
- **[Castanza2023]** Castanza AS, Recla JM, Eby D, Thorvaldsdottir H, Bult CJ, Mesirov JP. Extending support for mouse data in the Molecular Signatures Database (MSigDB). Nat Methods. 2023;20(11):1619-1620. doi:10.1038/s41592-023-02014-7. PMID: 37704782.
  MSigDB was human-only, with mouse data supported through basic ortholog mapping; Mouse MSigDB adds mouse-native gene sets because orthologs and paralogs are hard to discriminate and orthologous genes can differ in function; its MH collection is the ortholog-converted Hallmark set (full text).
- **[Danaher2017]** Danaher P, Warren S, Dennis L, et al. Gene expression markers of Tumor Infiltrating Leukocytes. J Immunother Cancer. 2017;5:18. doi:10.1186/s40425-017-0215-8. PMID: 28239471.
  Candidate marker lists from purified immune cells were evaluated by their co-expression in 9986 TCGA samples, which eliminated numerous false positives and left 60 marker genes for 14 populations; the cell scores were compared with flow cytometry and immunohistochemistry (abstract).
- **[Goeman2007]** Goeman JJ, Buhlmann P. Analyzing gene expression data in terms of gene sets: methodological issues. Bioinformatics. 2007;23(8):980-987. doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btm051. PMID: 17303618.
  Competitive tests compare a gene set with the other genes, self-contained tests test the set on its own; p-values from models that take genes as the sampling unit assume independence between genes and can be wildly anti-conservative (abstract).
- **[Hanzelmann2013]** Hanzelmann S, Castelo R, Guinney J. GSVA: gene set variation analysis for microarray and RNA-seq data. BMC Bioinformatics. 2013;14:7. doi:10.1186/1471-2105-14-7. PMID: 23323831.
  GSVA estimates variation of pathway activity over a sample population (abstract); ssGSEA, from Barbie et al., computes a per-sample enrichment statistic from expression ranks and normalises it by the range of values across all gene sets and samples (full text).
- **[Liberzon2015]** Liberzon A, Birger C, Thorvaldsdottir H, Ghandi M, Mesirov JP, Tamayo P. The Molecular Signatures Database (MSigDB) hallmark gene set collection. Cell Syst. 2015;1(6):417-425. doi:10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004. PMID: 26771021.
  Redundancy across and heterogeneity within gene sets reduce the utility of MSigDB; each hallmark is a refined set, derived from several founder sets, that displays coherent expression (abstract). There are 50 hallmarks; each was checked in an independent dataset not used for its refinement; redundancy takes the form of shared genes or of partial overlaps that read out the same process (full text).
- **[Merico2010]** Merico D, Isserlin R, Stueker O, Emili A, Bader GD. Enrichment Map: a network-based method for gene-set enrichment visualization and interpretation. PLoS One. 2010;5(11):e13984. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0013984. PMID: 21085593.
  Gene-set redundancy hampers interpretation (abstract); overlap between sets is measured by the Jaccard or the overlap coefficient, the latter preferred when hierarchical collections such as GO are included; the use cases set the overlap coefficient cut-off to 0.5 (full text).
- **[Nygaard2016]** Nygaard V, Rodland EA, Hovig E. Methods that remove batch effects while retaining group differences may lead to exaggerated confidence in downstream analyses. Biostatistics. 2016;17(1):29-39. doi:10.1093/biostatistics/kxv027. PMID: 26272994.
  Batch adjustment that preserves group differences (group and batch estimated together) can induce incorrect group differences downstream when groups are unevenly distributed across batches (abstract).
- **[Phipson2010]** Phipson B, Smyth GK. Permutation p-values should never be zero: calculating exact p-values when permutations are randomly drawn. Stat Appl Genet Mol Biol. 2010;9(1). doi:10.2202/1544-6115.1585. PMID: 21044043.
  Permutation and Monte Carlo p-values are often understated by about 1/m, m being the number of random draws; the draws should be treated as an exact discrete null distribution (abstract).
- **[Reimand2019]** Reimand J, Isserlin R, Voisin V, et al. Pathway enrichment analysis and visualization of omics data using g:Profiler, GSEA, Cytoscape and EnrichmentMap. Nat Protoc. 2019;14(2):482-517. doi:10.1038/s41596-018-0103-9. PMID: 30664679.
  A GMT file is text in which each line defines a pathway by its name, an identifier and its genes, and its gene identifiers must match those of the input; for human gene expression data the authors often recommend excluding pathway gene sets with fewer than 10-15 or more than 200-500 genes (upper bounds of 200-2,000 occur in the literature), and larger sets may be needed for non-human organisms; GSEA's default upper size limit is 500; Fisher's exact test-based methods favour larger pathways; EnrichmentMap similarity presets are Jaccard > 0.35, Jaccard > 0.25, combined > 0.375, overlap > 0.5 and overlap > 0.25 (full text).
- **[Venet2011]** Venet D, Dumont JE, Detours V. Most random gene expression signatures are significantly associated with breast cancer outcome. PLoS Comput Biol. 2011;7(10):e1002240. doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002240. PMID: 22028643.
  Signatures of postprandial laughter, mouse social defeat and skin fibroblast localisation were associated with breast cancer outcome; 28 of 47 published outcome signatures (60%) were not better than random signatures of identical size and 11 (23%) were worse than the median random signature; more than 90% of random signatures of over 100 genes were significant outcome predictors; adjusting for meta-PCNA (the 1% of genes most correlated with PCNA in normal tissues) almost abolished these associations, a signature's hazard ratio correlated with meta-PCNA (R^2 = 0.9), more than 50% of the breast cancer transcriptome correlated with meta-PCNA, and purging cell cycle genes out of a signature failed to rule out the confounding effect of proliferation (abstract). MSigDB c2 sets were benchmarked against random sets of identical size, and adjustment with permuted meta-PCNA values did not change outcome association (full text).
- **[Ziemann2016]** Ziemann M, Eren Y, El-Osta A. Gene name errors are widespread in the scientific literature. Genome Biol. 2016;17(1):177. doi:10.1186/s13059-016-1044-7. PMID: 27552985.
  Excel with default settings converts gene names to dates and floating-point numbers; about one-fifth of papers with supplementary Excel gene lists contained such errors (abstract). Examples: SEPT2 to '2-Sep' or '2006/09/02', MARCH1 to '1-Mar', RIKEN accession '2310009E13' to '2.31E+13' (full text).

## Software and databases

- **[Wang2026]** Wang A. tmelens: a transparent toolkit to profile the tumour immune microenvironment from bulk transcriptomes. Software, version 0.1.0, released 2026-10-17. MIT licence. https://github.com/Alex-w0731/tmelens
  Read directly (November 2026): `src/tmelens/signatures.py`, `src/tmelens/data/signatures.json`, `docs/signatures.md`, `scripts/make_signature_docs.py`. Source of the `published` / `curated` labels and their meaning, the signature record fields, the `original_symbols` mapping (CTGF to CCN2, FAM101B to RFLNB, KIAA0125 to FAM30A), the note that tmelens scores the TIS genes without the assay's weights, and `read_gmt` / `write_gmt` behaviour (lines with fewer than three fields skipped, the last of duplicated names kept, gene whitespace stripped; description written as the DOI, else the source label, else `user`). Re-check in later versions.
- **[HGNC2026]** HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee. HGNC database of human gene symbols. https://www.genenames.org (queried through https://rest.genenames.org, November 2026).
  Every previous symbol and alias in the built-in tables of `gmt_lint.py` was queried (re-checked 2026-11-20 at review: DEC1 is the previous symbol of DELEC1 and an alias of BHLHE40; SEPT2 is the previous symbol of SEPTIN2 and an alias of SEPTIN6; CD8B1 is a previous symbol of CD8B, whose record links MGI:88347, mouse Cd8b1); the complete-set download (https://storage.googleapis.com/public-download-files/hgnc/tsv/tsv/hgnc_complete_set.txt) had the columns `symbol`, `status`, `alias_symbol` and `prev_symbol` when read in November 2026.
- **[MGI2026]** Mouse Genome Informatics. Guidelines for Nomenclature of Genes, Genetic Markers, Alleles, and Mutations in Mouse and Rat. https://www.informatics.jax.org/mgihome/nomen/gene.shtml (accessed November 2026).
  Mouse gene symbols begin with an upper-case letter followed by lower-case letters or numbers, with stated exceptions; protein symbols are all upper case.
