The reply says that a purity estimate computed from immune or stromal gene expression (such as ESTIMATE) would be partly circular as a covariate for immune scores, and asks for one that does not depend on those genes, such as a DNA copy-number estimate (for example ABSOLUTE) or pathology review. Recommending ESTIMATE, or an unspecified purity estimate, without this caveat fails.
